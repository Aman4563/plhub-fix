## 🎨 Design System

### **Color Palette**

**Brand Colors:**
- Primary Red: `#e50914` → `#b20710` (gradient)
- Dark: `#0f0f0f` → `#1a1a1a` (gradient)
- Light: `#ffffff` → `#f8f9fa`

**Status Colors:**
- Success: `#10b981` → `#059669` (green gradient)
- Warning: `#f59e0b` → `#d97706` (orange gradient)
- Danger: `#dc2626` (red)
- Info: `#0ea5e9` (blue)

**Social Media Colors:**
- Twitter: `#1DA1F2` → `#0d8bd9`
- Instagram: `#E1306C` → `#C13584`
- YouTube: `#FF0000` → `#cc0000`
- LinkedIn: `#0077b5`

### **Typography**

**Font Family:** Inter (from Google Fonts)
- Weights: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semibold), 700 (Bold), 800 (Extrabold)

**Font Sizes:**
- H1: 32px (Header)
- H2: 28px (Main title)
- H3: 20-22px (Section headers)
- Body: 16px
- Small: 13-14px
- Ticket/Badge: 24px (monospace)

### **Spacing**

- Container padding: 40px (desktop), 24px (mobile)
- Section spacing: 32-48px
- Card padding: 24-32px
- Button padding: 16px 40px

### **Border Radius**

- Container: 16px
- Cards: 12px
- Buttons: 50px (pill shape)
- Badges: 20px
- Small elements: 6-8px

### **Shadows**

- Container: `0 20px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`
- Buttons: `0 8px 24px rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.3)`
- Tickets: `0 4px 16px rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.15)`
- Cards: `0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`
